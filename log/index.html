L006 · Recover the original data for scientific insight as well as reproducible figures

2026-10-16 · decision

Josu asked whether the data feeding the figures would support deeper scientific analysis, in addition to reproducing them in Matplotlib and LaTeX, and confirmed that he will ask Irene for the files. Prioritize the numerical data behind both figures, plotting scripts, native sample and reference measurements, calibration records and the definition of the shaded bands. Request individual acquisitions and independent bed-preparation repeats where available. The proposed analysis will first reproduce the existing curves and resolve their identities, including the ST4 panel discrepancy. It will then quantify sample and preparation differences, examine calibration and uncertainty with relevant correlations, assess reliable spectral intervals, and investigate reproducible spectral features alongside sample characterization. Independent preparations and a stated tolerance are needed before claiming preparation equivalence; the figures alone do not establish it. One documented reduction pipeline should feed the paper figures, numerical tables and website views. The plan is recorded on the From figures to data page; quantitative reanalysis is pending receipt of the measurements. The distinct indigo, ivory and copper website palette is recorded in L005. Desktop and phone checks passed for all twelve pages, and the 26 checked text colour pairs met a 4.5:1 contrast threshold. Josu explicitly requested that this discussion be retained in the project log.

Scientific review record

Date: 16 October 2026. This log documents the review and additions; it is not an experimental laboratory record.

Scope and preservation

  • Inventoried the original project: one LaTeX manuscript, one bibliography, two figure PDFs and .DS_Store.
  • Recorded file sizes and SHA-256 hashes before authoring in evidence/original_manifest.json.
  • Kept all additions in analysis_2026-10-16/. No original figure, manuscript, CV or thesis was edited.
  • Checked for applicable AGENTS.md instructions; none were found in this project or its checked ancestors.
  • Used a brief prior-memory check to identify earlier emissivity-definition pitfalls. Substantive conclusions were checked against the present draft, local thesis and primary sources.

Scientific study

  • Read the full draft and bibliography; rendered and inspected both original figures.
  • Extracted and searched the complete thesis; studied the relevant definitions, uncertainty, integration, validation and reporting sections. Visually checked critical PDF pages to avoid relying on conversion errors.
  • Located the 2025 and July 2026 CVs and older comprehensive publication list; cross-checked selected publications against primary sources.
  • Found and recorded a potential ST4 curve/label mismatch, undefined shaded bands, wavelength units error and case-sensitive figure filename mismatch.
  • Asked for sample identity and raw-data location while continuing independent work. No answers were available when the initial deliverables were prepared.

Literature verification

  • Consulted primary literature on infrared directional-hemispherical reflectance, granular quartz, soil composition/texture and moisture, plus selected coauthored publications.
  • Checked the official BIPM GUM listing and manufacturer information on atmospheric absorption and Infragold range.
  • Recorded successful and failed retrievals separately; three external PDFs were actually obtained and signature/type-verified.
  • Two downloaded Salisbury PDFs are scans. Relevant pages were rendered and read; empty text extraction was not treated as a full reading.
  • Crossref metadata verified nine DOI records. One rate-limited request was not retried; its bibliographic details were checked on the article itself.
  • Did not fit mineral abundances, digitize plots as experimental data, or assign laboratory uncertainty numbers.

Additions

  • Wrote a scientific review with 22 findings, model derivation, interpretation, thesis/publication crosswalk, and a prioritized completion route.
  • Wrote a separate expanded LaTeX draft with abstract, introduction, methods, covariance-aware uncertainty expression, results, discussion, conclusions and author queries.
  • Added a 12-entry bibliography; optional entries are not forced into the article.
  • Retained original figures unmodified and disclosed their unresolved issues in the new captions.
  • Added an experimental information checklist and a source inventory with explicit reading/retrieval limits.
  • Added reproducible illustrative calculations for Planck coverage, absolute/relative uncertainty and common-mode covariance cancellation. These are not sample results.

Verification

The final machine-readable results are in evidence/validation.json. Verification covers source preservation, bibliography/cross-references, successful LaTeX compilation, numerical checks and PDF rendering. Final visual inspection and any remaining layout notes are recorded there. The review does not certify measurement accuracy without the raw records and missing experimental information.

Reproduction

From this directory:

python3 evidence/check_calculations.py
python3 evidence/build_bibliography.py
cd revised
latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main_revised.tex

The bibliography builder uses saved metadata and does not require fresh network requests. Figures are referenced from the unchanged project root; keep the relative directory structure when moving the manuscript. The original retrieval scripts are an audit trail, not a recommendation to repeat blocked requests.
